About

I'm a senior product designer with eight years at ConstructConnect, working on search and discovery in B2B SaaS. My home base is the OptiSearch squad on CCPI, ConstructConnect's project intelligence platform, where the work spans guided onboarding, document experiences, and AI-assisted search.

The through-line in my work is the decisions between the artifacts: the tradeoffs, constraints, and judgment calls that determine what actually ships. I care about validating before building, degrading gracefully instead of hiding features, and making AI explain itself rather than perform magic.

Outside of work I'm writing and drawing a 32-page horror graphic novel, running, and keeping up with my kids.
