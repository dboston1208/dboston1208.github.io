How I work

  1. 1
    Validate before building

    Every case study here starts with what users did, not what the team assumed. Early testing is cheaper than a wrong sprint.

  2. 2
    Degrade gracefully, don’t hide features

    When a user lacks access, the feature should still explain itself. Empty and disabled states are design, not edge cases.

  3. 3
    Make AI explain itself

    An AI feature people can’t audit is one they won’t use twice. Show the interpretation and let them correct it.
