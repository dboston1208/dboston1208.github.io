The Problem

Powerful, but only once you know how

New CCPI users struggled to get started on their own. Without guidance, they either leaned heavily on ConstructConnect reps to set up their first saved searches, or left before finding value.

This created a user-side problem (a confusing first experience that eroded trust) and a business-side problem (activation depending on human intervention isn't scalable).

CCPI's value only becomes visible once a user completes a meaningful search. Everything in onboarding needed to move them toward that moment faster.

Key Design Decisions

Activation over education

The temptation in onboarding is to teach users everything. The decision here was the opposite: get users to one successful outcome first, then expand.

Designed for the user who doesn't have a rep

Activators handles high-touch onboarding for enterprise accounts. This flow was built for users who don't get that — smaller accounts, self-serve signups, or moments when a rep isn't available. That shaped the tone and hand-holding in each step.

Cross-team handoff as a design constraint

Because Activators owned the relationship and OptiSearch owned the surface, the flow had to work independently of either team's direct involvement.

Pendo as the delivery layer

What worked
